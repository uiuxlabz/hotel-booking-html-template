World-Class Services

From rejuvenating spa treatments to unforgettable culinary journeys, every service is designed to exceed expectations.

◆

Luxury Spa & Wellness

Surrender to tranquility in our award-winning spa. Featuring 12 treatment rooms, a thermal suite, and signature therapies using organic botanicals sourced from around the world.

◆

Fine Dining

Three distinct dining experiences await — from our Michelin-starred The Ashford Room to the intimate Rooftop Terrace, each offering a journey through global flavors and local ingredients.

◆

Private Events

Create unforgettable moments in our elegant venues. From intimate gatherings in the Garden Room to grand galas in the Heritage Ballroom, every event receives bespoke attention.

◆

Butler Service

Suite guests enjoy the devoted attention of a personal butler — from unpacking luggage to arranging private excursions, every detail is handled with discretion and care.

◆

Airport Transfer

Begin and end your journey in style with our luxury chauffeur service. Complimentary for suite guests, available for all visitors upon request.

◆

Kids Club

Young guests are treated to their own adventure in our supervised Kids Club, featuring creative activities, storytelling sessions, and educational workshops.
