Our Heritage

A Legacy of Timeless Elegance

Since 1927, Grandehotel has been the benchmark of luxury hospitality. Founded by the Ashford family, our hotel has hosted royalty, dignitaries, and discerning travelers from around the world.

Every detail — from the hand-selected marble in our lobbies to the personalized service of our concierge — reflects nearly a century of dedication to the art of hospitality.
