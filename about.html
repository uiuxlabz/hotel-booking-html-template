Our Story

A Century of Elegance

In 1927, Charles Ashford opened the doors of what would become the city's most iconic hotel. His vision was simple yet revolutionary: create a place where every guest feels like royalty, where luxury is not just seen but felt in every detail.

Through the golden age of travel, through decades of change, Grandehotel has remained a beacon of refined hospitality. Three generations of the Ashford family have upheld the founding principles — impeccable service, timeless design, and an unwavering commitment to excellence.

Today, under the stewardship of Eleanor Ashford, Grandehotel continues to write new chapters while honoring its storied past. We blend heritage with innovation, ensuring every guest experiences the perfect balance of tradition and modern luxury.
